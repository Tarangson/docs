## Overview

The Dashboard is the first page you see after signing in. It consists of three main sections: the Header, category menu cards, and a settings button. Every feature in the system is accessible from this page.

---

## Header

The header section contains:

- **Tarangson logo** -- Located on the left side
- **User profile menu** -- Located on the right side, displaying your profile picture or initials from your email

### User Profile Menu

Click your profile picture in the top-right corner to open a dropdown menu with the following options:

| Menu Item | Description |
|---|---|
| Username and email | Displays your name and email address |
| **Settings** (ตั้งค่า) | Go to the school and account settings page |
| **Billing** (การชำระเงิน) | Go to the billing settings page |
| **Sign Out** (ออกจากระบบ) | Sign out and return to the sign-in page |

## Account Settings Button

At the bottom of the Dashboard, there is an **Account Settings** (ตั้งค่าแอคเคาท์) button that takes you to the settings page for your school, address, contact, and billing information.

---

## Recommended Steps After Reaching the Dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure timetable defaults">
    Click the **Timetable Defaults** (ตารางเรียนพื้นฐาน) card to set your school days, number of periods, and the time for each period. See the [Academic Defaults](/settings/academic-defaults) page for more details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add teachers, students, classrooms, and subjects">
    Enter all four types of basic data before you start scheduling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start scheduling">
    Click the **Start New Timetable** (เริ่มจัดตารางสอนใหม่) card to create a timetable file and begin scheduling.
  </Step>
</Steps>
