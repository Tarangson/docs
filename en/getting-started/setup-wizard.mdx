## Overview

When you sign in for the first time, the system will automatically take you to the Setup Wizard. The wizard consists of 4 steps that you complete in order. Once you finish all steps, you will be taken to the Dashboard and can start scheduling right away.

### All Steps

| Step | Details |
|---|---|
| 1. Choose a Plan | Select a plan that fits your school size |
| 2. School Information | Enter your school name, type, grade levels, colors, and logo |
| 3. Address | Enter your school address and billing address |
| 4. Contact | Enter the subscriber's information and school contact details |

## Step 2: School Information

Enter your school's basic information. This data will be used when displaying timetables and generating reports.

### School Name

| Field | Required | Example |
|---|---|---|
| School Name (Thai) | Yes | โรงเรียนสาธิตแห่งมหาวิทยาลัย |
| School Name (English) | No | Bangkok Demo School |
| Abbreviation (Thai) | Yes | สธ.มศว |
| Abbreviation (English) | No | BDS |

<Callout type="info" title="Abbreviations are automatically uppercased">
  Regardless of whether you type in lowercase or uppercase, the system will automatically convert abbreviations to uppercase.
</Callout>

### School Type (Required)

Select your school type from the following options:

- Government (รัฐบาล)
- Private (เอกชน)
- International (นานาชาติ)
- Demonstration (สาธิต)
- Municipal / Provincial (เทศบาล / อบจ.)
- Other (อื่นๆ)

### Grade Levels Offered (Required)

You can select more than one level by checking the boxes next to each grade level your school offers:

- Kindergarten (อนุบาล)
- Primary (ประถมศึกษา)
- Lower Secondary (มัธยมศึกษาตอนต้น)
- Upper Secondary (มัธยมศึกษาตอนปลาย)
- Vocational (อาชีวศึกษา)

### School Size (Optional)

| Size | Number of Students |
|---|---|
| Small (ขนาดเล็ก) | Up to 119 |
| Medium (ขนาดกลาง) | 120 -- 719 |
| Large (ขนาดใหญ่) | 720 -- 1,679 |
| Extra Large (ขนาดใหญ่พิเศษ) | 1,680 or more |

### School Colors and Logo (Optional)

You can set your school colors (primary and secondary) by choosing from a preset color palette or entering a HEX color code directly, such as `#1E40AF`.

For the school logo, the system supports PNG, JPEG, and SVG files.

When you are done, click **Continue** (ดำเนินการต่อ).

## Step 3: Address

### School Address

Enter your school's address, which includes:

- **Address** -- Street number, village, road, alley
- **Sub-district** (ตำบล/แขวง) -- Supports Thai autocomplete
- **District** (อำเภอ/เขต) -- Supports Thai autocomplete
- **Province** (จังหวัด) -- Supports Thai autocomplete
- **Postal Code** (รหัสไปรษณีย์) -- Supports Thai autocomplete

<Callout type="tip" title="Thai address autocomplete">
  The address fields feature Thai address autocomplete. When you start typing a sub-district, district, or province name, the system will suggest matching entries. When you select one, the remaining address fields will be filled in automatically. This feature uses Thailand's administrative division database to speed up data entry.
</Callout>

### Billing Address

By default, the billing address uses the same information as the school address. If you need a separate billing address, uncheck the **Same as school address** (เหมือนที่อยู่สถานศึกษา) checkbox and enter the new address.

You can also enter your **Tax ID Number** (เลขผู้เสียภาษี) (13 digits) in this section.

## Step 4: Contact

### Subscriber Information (Required)

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Full Name (ชื่อ-นามสกุล) | Yes | Name of the person registering the system |
| Position (ตำแหน่ง) | Yes | e.g., Head of Academic Affairs |
| Phone Number (เบอร์โทรศัพท์) | Yes | Format: 0XXXXXXXXX (10 digits) |
| Email (อีเมล) | Yes | Contact email address |

### School Contact Person

By default, the school contact person uses the same information as the subscriber. If you need a different contact person, uncheck **Use same info as subscriber** (ใช้ข้อมูลเดียวกับผู้สมัคร) and enter the new details.

### Online Channels (Optional)

You can add online contact channels, including:

- **Website** of the school
- **LINE OA ID** (a popular messaging platform in Thailand)
- **Facebook Page**

When you are done, click **Finish** (เสร็จสิ้น) to enter the Dashboard.

---

## After Setup

Once you have completed all steps, the system will take you to the [Dashboard](/getting-started/dashboard) immediately. From there, you can start configuring your basic timetable settings and begin scheduling.

<Callout type="tip" title="Edit later">
  All information you entered during setup can be edited later through the [School Profile](/settings/school-profile) settings page.
</Callout>
