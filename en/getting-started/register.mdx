## Overview

The Tarangson sign-in page supports three modes: sign in with email/password, register a new account, and reset your password. You can also sign in instantly with your Google account.

The system uses `Firebase Authentication` to manage all user accounts.

---

## Register with Email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the registration page">
    Go to the sign-in page and click the **Register** (สมัครสมาชิก) link at the bottom of the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your details">
    Enter the email address and password you want to use, then re-enter your password in the **Confirm Password** (ยืนยันรหัสผ่าน) field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Register">
    Click the **Register** (สมัครสมาชิก) button to create your account. The system will automatically redirect you to the school setup page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Password requirements">
  Your password must be at least 6 characters long, and both password fields must match.
</Callout>

---

## Sign In with Email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your email and password">
    Enter the email and password you used when registering.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Sign In">
    Click the **Sign In** (เข้าสู่ระบบ) button. The system will verify your credentials and take you to the Dashboard if your school is already set up, or to the Setup Wizard if it is not.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Sign In with Google

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the Google button">
    On the sign-in or registration page, click **Sign in with Google** (เข้าสู่ระบบด้วย Google) or **Register with Google** (สมัครด้วย Google).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Google account">
    A Google pop-up window will appear. Select the account you want to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign-in complete">
    If this is your first time, the system will automatically create an account for you and redirect you to the school setup page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Pop-up blocked?">
  If your browser blocks the pop-up, you will see a warning message. Allow pop-ups for this website and try again.
</Callout>

---

## Forgot Password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the forgot password link">
    On the sign-in page, click the **Forgot password?** (ลืมรหัสผ่าน?) link below the password field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Enter the email address you used when registering.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send Reset Link">
    Click the **Send Reset Link** (ส่งลิงก์รีเซ็ต) button. The system will send an email with a password reset link to your inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a new password">
    Open the email you received, click the password reset link, and set your new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Didn't receive the email?">
  Check your spam or junk folder. If you still can't find it, try sending the reset link again.
</Callout>

## Common Errors

| Error | Cause |
|---|---|
| Invalid email format | The email address you entered is not in a valid format |
| This email is already in use | An account with this email already exists -- try signing in instead |
| Incorrect email or password | The credentials you entered do not match any account |
| Password must be at least 6 characters | The password you entered is too short |
| Too many requests | You have tried signing in too many times -- wait a moment and try again |
| Network error | Check your internet connection |

---

## After Signing In

After you successfully sign in, the system checks whether you have already set up your school:

- **Already set up** -- You are taken to the [Dashboard](/getting-started/dashboard)
- **Not yet set up** -- You are taken to the [Setup Wizard](/getting-started/setup-wizard) to get started
