## Overview

Tarangson uses `Firebase Authentication` to manage user accounts. You can view your account information and manage your profile through the menu in the top-right corner of every page.

---

## User Profile Information

You can view your account details by clicking your profile picture in the top-right corner. The dropdown menu displays:

- **Username** -- Shows the name you have set, or the first part of your email (before the @ sign)
- **Email** -- The email address you used to register or sign in with Google

### Profile Picture

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Signed in with Google">
    The system automatically uses your Google account's profile picture.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Signed in with Email">
    The system displays the first letter of your email address in uppercase as the default profile picture.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Subscriber Information

Your personal details as the system subscriber (separate from your Firebase account credentials) can be edited on the settings page under the **Contact** (ผู้ติดต่อ) tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the settings page">
    Click **Settings** (ตั้งค่า) from the profile menu in the top-right corner, or click **Account Settings** (ตั้งค่าแอคเคาท์) on the Dashboard page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Contact tab">
    Click the **Contact** (ผู้ติดต่อ) tab in the left sidebar (or the tab bar at the top on mobile).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your information">
    Update the fields you need:

    - **Full Name** (ชื่อ-นามสกุล) -- Name of the system subscriber
    - **Position** (ตำแหน่ง) -- Your position at the school
    - **Phone Number** (เบอร์โทรศัพท์) -- Contact number (format: `0XXXXXXXXX`)
    - **Email** (อีเมล) -- Contact email address
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click the **Save** (บันทึก) button to confirm your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Subscriber info and sign-in account are separate">
  Editing the name and email in the Contact section does not affect your sign-in account. The information here is your school's contact data, not your `Firebase` account credentials.
</Callout>

## Reset Password

If you registered with email and want to change your password, you can do so through the forgot password feature:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign out">
    Click **Sign Out** (ออกจากระบบ) from the profile menu in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Forgot Password">
    On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?** (ลืมรหัสผ่าน?).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Enter the email address you used to register, then click **Send Reset Link** (ส่งลิงก์รีเซ็ต).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a new password">
    Open the email you received, click the password reset link, and set your new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Signed in with Google?">
  If you sign in with Google, you do not need to manage a password on Tarangson. The system authenticates directly through your Google account.
</Callout>

---

## Sign Out

You can sign out in two ways:

1. **From the profile menu** -- Click your profile picture in the top-right corner, then select **Sign Out** (ออกจากระบบ) (displayed in red text)
2. **From the settings page** -- Click **Sign Out** (ออกจากระบบ) in the profile menu on the settings page

After signing out, you will be returned to the sign-in page immediately.

## Profile Menu Summary

The profile menu in the top-right corner contains the following items (options may vary slightly depending on which page you are on):

### From the Dashboard

| Menu Item | Action |
|---|---|
| Settings (ตั้งค่า) | Go to the school settings page |
| Billing (การชำระเงิน) | Go to the billing tab in settings |
| Sign Out (ออกจากระบบ) | Sign out of the system |

### From the Settings Page

| Menu Item | Action |
|---|---|
| Home (หน้าหลัก) | Return to the Dashboard |
| Sign Out (ออกจากระบบ) | Sign out of the system |
