## Overview

The School Profile settings page brings together all information related to your school in one place. You can edit any of the data you entered during the Setup Wizard at any time.

You can access this page by:
- Clicking **Account Settings** (ตั้งค่าแอคเคาท์) at the bottom of the Dashboard
- Clicking **Settings** (ตั้งค่า) in the profile menu at the top-right corner
- Navigating to `/settings`

---

## Settings Page Structure

The settings page has a sidebar on the left (on mobile, it appears as a tab bar at the top) divided into two groups:

### School Settings

| Tab | Description |
|---|---|
| Plan (แพ็กเกจ) | Change your current plan |
| School Info (ข้อมูลโรงเรียน) | Edit school name, type, grade levels, colors, and logo |
| Address (ที่อยู่) | Edit school address and billing address |
| Contact (ผู้ติดต่อ) | Edit subscriber info and contact channels |

### Billing

| Tab | Description |
|---|---|
| Billing (การชำระเงิน) | Manage payments and view payment history |

## School Info

This section lets you edit your school's basic information -- the same data you entered in Step 2 of the Setup Wizard.

### Editable Fields

<Steps>
  <Step title="School Name">
    Edit the school name (Thai/English) and abbreviation (Thai/English). The Thai school name and Thai abbreviation are required fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type and Grade Levels">
    Change the school type (Government, Private, International, Demonstration, Municipal/Provincial, Other) and grade levels offered (Kindergarten, Primary, Lower Secondary, Upper Secondary, Vocational).
  </Step>
  <Step title="School Size">
    Select the school size (Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large) and enter the approximate number of students.
  </Step>
  <Step title="School Colors">
    Choose primary and secondary colors from a preset palette or enter HEX color codes directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="School Logo">
    Upload or change your school logo. Supported file types: PNG, JPEG, and SVG.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you are done editing, click **Save** (บันทึก).

## Address

### School Address

Edit all 5 address fields: Address, Sub-district (ตำบล/แขวง), District (อำเภอ/เขต), Province (จังหวัด), and Postal Code (รหัสไปรษณีย์). The sub-district, district, province, and postal code fields support Thai address autocomplete.

### Billing Address

By default, the billing address uses the same information as the school address. If you need a separate billing address, uncheck **Same as school address** (เหมือนที่อยู่สถานศึกษา) and enter the new address.

You can also enter your **Tax ID Number** (เลขผู้เสียภาษี) (optional, 13 digits) in this section.

When you are done editing, click **Save** (บันทึก).

## Contact

### Subscriber Information

| Field | Required |
|---|---|
| Full Name (ชื่อ-นามสกุล) | Yes |
| Position (ตำแหน่ง) | Yes |
| Phone Number (เบอร์โทรศัพท์) | Yes |
| Email (อีเมล) | Yes |

<Callout type="info" title="Phone number format">
  The phone number must start with 0 and contain 9-10 digits, e.g., `0812345678`. This follows the Thai telephone number format.
</Callout>

### School Contact Person

By default, the school contact person uses the same information as the subscriber. If you need a different contact person, uncheck **Use same info as subscriber** (ใช้ข้อมูลเดียวกับผู้สมัคร) and enter the new details.

### Online Channels (Optional)

- Website
- LINE OA ID (a popular messaging platform in Thailand)
- Facebook Page

When you are done editing, click **Save** (บันทึก).

## Billing

Access this section by clicking the **Billing** (การชำระเงิน) tab in the sidebar, or from the profile menu on the Dashboard.

### Credit / Debit Card

<Callout type="info" title="Coming soon">
  Credit/debit card payment via Stripe is not yet available.
</Callout>

### Bank Transfer

You can pay by transferring funds to the designated bank account and uploading a transfer slip (PNG or JPEG, max 5 MB).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Transfer funds">
    Transfer the specified amount to the bank account shown on screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the transfer slip">
    Click **Choose File** (เลือกไฟล์) to upload a photo of your transfer slip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit proof of payment">
    Click **Submit Transfer Proof** (ส่งหลักฐานการโอนเงิน) to confirm your payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Payment History

The payment history table displays all past transactions, including:

- Transaction ID
- Date and time
- Plan
- Amount
- Status (Successful / Trial)
